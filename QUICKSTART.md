# LET ME COOK: Quickstart

## 1. Load the skill

Download [SKILL.md](./SKILL.md) and provide its full contents to your LLM as project instructions, custom instructions, or an attached file with this instruction:

> Follow the attached LET ME COOK skill for this conversation. Activate it when I say `/let-me-cook` and respect the brain-dump listening mode.

Some platforms have native skills or project-specific instruction files; install or paste the Markdown according to the platform's guidance. A slash command is **not automatically registered** just because you upload a Markdown file.

## 2. Start cooking

Type `/let-me-cook` or `let me cook`, then freely dump thoughts. The AI must listen, not interrogate, until you say **DONE WHIPPIN**.

During your dump, type `SIDE DISH:` before an idea that could become its own project.

## 3. Finish the dump

Say **DONE WHIPPIN**. The assistant then decodes what you shared, identifies key assumptions and tensions, and asks targeted questions.

## 4. Refine and export

- **GRILL ME** for sharper questions.
- **LET IT MARINATE** to park a side dish.
- **SHOW THE RECIPE** for a working blueprint.
- **MAKE IT A PROMPT**, **MAKE IT AN AGENT**, or **MAKE IT A PLAN** for specific formats.
- **PLATE IT** for the finished output.

## Example

You: `/let-me-cook`

Assistant: `I'm listening. Dump the idea however it comes out. I won't interrupt until you say DONE WHIPPIN.`

You: `I want an online membership for creative founders... SIDE DISH: maybe a podcast too... DONE WHIPPIN`

Assistant: Summarizes the goal, separates the podcast tangent, and asks high-value questions before producing the plan.

## Important limitations

This is an LLM instruction file, not a standalone app. Memory, file access, voice support, external actions, and quality of execution depend on your model and tools. Do not share sensitive information unless you trust the provider and its data settings.
